Privacy Policy

Game: ASMR Animal Colors (Android Application) • Last Updated: October 2026
Core Privacy Guarantee:
ASMR Animal Colors is committed to protecting the privacy of our players and children. We do NOT collect, sell, or harvest any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as real names, phone numbers, home addresses, or physical locations.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ASMR Animal Colors ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by Suno Games Studio. We offer a soothing, creative digital coloring experience featuring animal illustrations, calming ASMR brush textures, and peaceful audio for children and players of all ages.

2. Children's Privacy & Google Play Families Policy

Our application adheres to the strict standards set forth by the Google Play Families Policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K):

  • Child-Directed Treatment: All advertising requests within the app explicitly pass the tagForChildDirectedTreatment=true and max_ad_content_rating=G flags.
  • No Behavioral Profiling: We do not track children across third-party websites or services, build advertising profiles, or serve interest-based/personalized advertising.
  • Parental Gate for External Links: Any external transitions (such as Store ratings or optional subscriptions) are secured to prevent inadvertent actions by young children.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free to play, we integrate Google AdMob, an official Google Play Certified Ad SDK:

  • Ad Formats: The Game may display optional rewarded video ads (to unlock drawings or hints) and infrequent interstitial ads during natural transitions between completed coloring pages.
  • Non-Personalized Ads Only: In accordance with Google's child-directed standards, advertisements served are strictly contextual and non-personalized. AdMob may process non-sensitive technical signals (such as device model, OS version, or anonymous ad-serving telemetry) purely for fraud detection, ad capping, and service delivery.
  • For further details regarding Google's ad privacy practices, please review Google AdMob Privacy & Technologies.

4. In-App Subscriptions & Google Play Billing

The Game offers optional VIP subscriptions to remove advertisements and instantly unlock all animal collections:

  • All purchases are securely handled exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing.
  • We never have access to, receive, or store credit card numbers, banking credentials, or billing addresses. All financial processing is performed by Google LLC in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Local Device Storage & Gallery Permissions

The Game stores non-personal functional variables on your device:

  • Game Progression & Cache: Unlocked categories, earned stars/coins, sound settings, and incomplete drawing canvas strokes are stored locally on your device via Android sandboxed storage. No personal data is transmitted off your phone.
  • Save Picture to Gallery: When you tap "Save Drawing", the app saves the finished image (PNG) directly to your device's photo gallery (/Pictures/ASMR Animal Colors/) using Android's standard MediaStore API. The app does not read, access, or modify your existing private photos.
  • Vibration / Haptics: Used strictly to provide tactile feedback while coloring with crayons and brushes.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You have total control over your game data:

  • You can reset current drawings using the in-game "Clear Picture" button.
  • You can delete all stored game progress at any time by clearing the app's data in Android System Settings (Settings → Apps → ASMR Animal Colors → Storage → Clear Data).
