Privacy Policy

Game: ASMR Animal Colors (YouTube Playables) • Effective Date: October 11, 2026
Core Privacy Guarantee:
ASMR Animal Colors does NOT collect, track, sell, or share personal data from any user. Designed for players of all ages, teens (13+), and adults seeking relaxing, anti-stress ASMR coloring without intrusive tracking or advertisements.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ASMR Animal Colors ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We create peaceful, creative digital art and ASMR relaxation experiences for players of all ages on the YouTube Playables platform. We respect your confidentiality and believe in providing a safe, distraction-free environment.

2. Platform Context & Age Appropriateness

  • YouTube Playables (13+ Platform): ASMR Animal Colors is delivered via YouTube Playables, which is operated by Google LLC for general audience users (ages 13 and older, or with supervised accounts).
  • All-Ages Content: The Game features relaxing animal illustrations, satisfying textures (crayons, modeling dough, yarn, sponges), and calming procedural audio. It contains no violence, mature themes, or adult content, making it universally suitable.
  • Minors & Children: Because we collect zero personal information from any player, we do not collect or maintain personal data from minors or children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), in full compliance with COPPA and GDPR.

3. Gameplay Data & Cloud Saves

The Game only handles non-personal, functional game states necessary for gameplay progression:

  • What is stored: Unlocked animal categories and levels, earned in-game reward coins, sound/music preferences, active UI theme, and incomplete coloring stroke data.
  • How it is stored: Stored securely via the official YouTube Playables SDK (window.ytgame.game.saveData) in Google's cloud infrastructure and locally in the player's sandboxed browser storage (localStorage).
  • Purpose: Strictly to allow you to resume your artwork and settings. This data is never transmitted to external servers or used for marketing or user profiling.

4. Device Permissions

The Game operates strictly within the browser sandbox and does not request or access:

  • ❌ Camera or video capture
  • ❌ Microphone or audio recording
  • ❌ GPS or precise geographic location
  • ❌ Contacts, address books, or external photo albums

5. Third-Party Services & YouTube Platform

The Game is hosted and delivered through the YouTube Playables service, operated by Google LLC. Any platform-level metrics or delivery services are managed directly by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

Players have full control over their game progress at all times. You can clear current artwork using the in-game "Clear Picture" option, or wipe local game state by clearing your browser cache/cookies for YouTube.

7. Compliance with Global Privacy Laws

Our zero-personal-data architecture is compliant with international privacy standards, including:

  • GDPR / UK GDPR: No personal data processing; no behavioral profiling.
  • CCPA / CPRA: Zero sale or sharing of personal information.
  • COPPA: Absolute non-collection of personal identifiers.
